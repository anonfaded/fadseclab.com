## What you can do with FadCam on iOS

- **Dashcam** — mount your phone and record the road. Live timestamp watermark and optional custom text overlay (e.g. license plate number).
- **Bodycam** — record hands-free with the display dimmed to save battery.
- **Dark screen mode** — record video with the screen effectively off, saving power on long sessions.
- **Zero telemetry** — no analytics, no tracking, no accounts required. Code is public under GPLv3.
